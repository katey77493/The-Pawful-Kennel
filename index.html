Because Pups Need Vacations Too!

Not everyday owners can satisfy their restless pups' demands. Whether it be a bubble bath, extra training or simply a restful retreat from mundane life, The Pawful Kennel is there for your pups.

The Pawful Kennel is a safe retreat for all dogs, ranging from little pups to seniors. All dog breeds are accepted!*

Our staff is trained and qualified to give your pets the best-pawtastic experience of their lives. We provide many services such as:

Grooming
Skilled in washing, drying, brushing teeth and clipping claws with high quality tools and fur-friendly products
Pet Sitting
Qualified in overseeing multiple pets at a time, along with tending to any incidents that may occur
Training
Qualified in training young dogs in basic to advance tricks and commands

Certain breeds such as Pit Bull-type, Rottweiler and Doberman Pinscher must be trained prior to reservation. Pets in this list should be accompanied with a muzzel if <5 months or untrained. The Pawful Kennel will not accept any dog that is aggressive upon arrival.
